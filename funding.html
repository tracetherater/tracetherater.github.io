Funding & Partnerships

Trace The Rater is seeking long-term, sustainable funding for independent media and lifestyle journalism. We publish original reviews, reporting, videos, and commentary on television, film, games, music, and the culture around them.

A reliable funding base gives us time to research, produce, and publish consistently. Over time, it can help TTR grow from a one-person publication into an independent media company with writers, journalists, presenters, and producers.

Discuss funding or support

Ways to fund TTR

Sponsorships and advertising

We welcome recurring sponsorships and clearly labeled advertising from businesses that want to help sustain original media production.

Institutional funding and grants

We welcome conversations with foundations and other institutions about multi-year funding, eligible grants, and support for specific reporting or production projects.

Audience support

If you’d like to contribute once or on a recurring basis, email us to ask about available options. Sharing our work and subscribing to the newsletter also help people discover TTR.

What funding supports

  • Purchasing and accessing the media we cover, plus research and fact-checking.
  • Production time, equipment, software, and editing for original articles and videos.
  • Website hosting, publishing, and day-to-day business operations.
  • Paid contributions from writers, researchers, and producers as TTR grows.

Editorial independence

Trace The Rater retains final authority over what we cover, how we cover it, and the opinions and ratings we publish. Funding agreements confer no editorial approval, favorable coverage, or higher ratings. We clearly label advertising and sponsored material and disclose relevant financial relationships when they relate to our coverage.

Start a funding conversation

Tell us what kind of support you’re considering, your preferred timeline, and how to reach you. Organizations can use the existing collaboration inquiry form. Individuals can email TTR about audience support.
