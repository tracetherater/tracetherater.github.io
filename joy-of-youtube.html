The Joy of YouTube: How Free Entertainment Enhances Everyday Life

By Trace Thompkins | Trace The Rater
November 19, 2026

Watch the video · Read the article

Watch: Why YouTube Makes My Everyday Life Better!

DING DING DING! It’s Trace The Rater time!

One of my favorite things about YouTube is the pleasure I get from having something playing in the background while I go about my everyday life. Sometimes I watch closely; other times I listen while doing something else. There is always another creative perspective to discover.

Entertainment That Fits Into My Day

I enjoy watching reviews, stories, and creative work about television, film, gaming, music, food, mental health, and other relatable subjects. YouTube gives me the freedom to switch between those interests without having to follow a fixed television schedule.

A creator might share an opinion about a movie, explain what makes a video game interesting, or tell a story about their own experiences. I particularly enjoy hearing different perspectives on subjects that interest me and discovering how other creators approach their work. Not every video needs my full attention, but when something catches my interest, I can give it a closer look.

A Place for Classic Television, Too

Another appealing aspect of YouTube is the availability of full episodes and compilations that rights holders make available without a subscription. As an animation enthusiast, I especially enjoy revisiting cartoons associated with Cartoon Network in the 1990s whenever they are legitimately available. It is a convenient way to discover and appreciate older entertainment alongside newer productions.

Free viewing does not necessarily mean every show will remain available forever. Catalogs change, and I value legal options that preserve the original presentation of a program. I discussed a related idea in my article about the future of free television.

Why I Value Independent Creators

YouTube is also an important source of inspiration as I develop Trace The Rater. I appreciate how independent creators can turn a passion for entertainment or everyday experiences into something meaningful for an audience. Their work reminds me why I want to keep creating my own reviews, commentary, and stories.

Supporting the Future of Trace The Rater

Through Trace The Rater, I want to explore television, film, music, gaming, and lifestyle topics from a wide range of perspectives. Sustainable funding could help me continue producing original journalism and entertainment content, including research and lawful access to media from companies such as Skydance, Disney, NBCUniversal, Sony, and Netflix, as well as work from independent online creators.

When possible, I would love to build a lasting collection of media that I can revisit for future reviews and analysis. Funding is not a guarantee of permanent access, but it could give me more flexibility in selecting and covering the entertainment I care about.

To learn more about the project and its funding goals, visit the Trace The Rater funding page.

What Do You Watch in the Background?

For me, YouTube is more than a place to watch videos. It is a flexible source of entertainment, creativity, and discovery that can accompany everyday activities. What kinds of videos do you enjoy playing while going about your day?
