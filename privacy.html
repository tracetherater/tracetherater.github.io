Privacy & analytics choices

Optional analytics helps us understand page visits, social-link clicks and business-plan PDF-link clicks. Analytics collection remains inactive until a Google Analytics property is configured.

Your choice

When configured, Google Analytics loads only after you allow it. A preference is stored in this browser. Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track signals prevent loading. Declining does not prevent you from using the website.

What is measured

Analytics reports are private. We do not publish subscriber records, private messages, financial records or analytics dashboards. Our custom events send the page path, destination hostname or PDF path; they do not send social profile query strings or contact-form content. Google may process device, browser, network and cookie identifiers when analytics is allowed.

Link clicks do not prove that someone followed an account or finished reading a PDF. Visits blocked by browser settings or declined consent will be absent from reports.
