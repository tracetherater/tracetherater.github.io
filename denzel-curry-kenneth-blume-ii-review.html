Denzel Curry & Kenneth Blume's ii Is My Favorite Album of 2026 — 100/100 Review

By Trace Thompkins | Trace The Rater
November 11, 2026

Watch the video review · Read the written review

Watch my video review

Overall Rating: 100/100

DING DING DING! It's Trace The Rater time!

Sometimes an album comes along that completely blows me away. Whether it's the production, the sampling, the featured artists or simply the overall listening experience, certain albums remind me of why I love reviewing music.

For me, Denzel Curry and Kenneth Blume's ii is one of those albums.

I'm not exaggerating when I say this: ii is my favorite album of 2026, my favorite album of the 2020s so far, and one of my favorite albums of all time!

Let's talk about why.

A Reunion Worth Celebrating

Released on August 21, 2026, ii is the follow-up to Denzel Curry and producer Kenny Beats' 2020 collaborative project UNLOCKED.

The nine-track project runs approximately 24 minutes and features appearances from JPEGMAFIA, Westside Gunn and Yebba.

Despite its relatively short runtime, ii has made an enormous impression on me.

Kenny's Production Absolutely Slaps!

If there's one thing I especially appreciate about this album, it's Kenneth Blume's production.

The beats are incredible, the sampling is epic, and the musical ideas make this project feel special to me.

Production is one of the most important parts of my music reviews because it can significantly affect how enjoyable a song is.

On ii, Kenny delivers the kind of production that makes me want to revisit the music repeatedly.

And when you combine those beats with Denzel Curry's performances, you get a collaboration that I find tremendously enjoyable.

Great Features Make the Album Even Better

Another major strength of ii is its featured artists.

The album includes three guest appearances:

  • JPEGMAFIA on "EVIL GRIN"
  • Westside Gunn on "CROWN"
  • Yebba on "DIFFERENCE"

I appreciate the variety these artists bring to the project. Each appearance contributes to the album's overall identity.

These collaborations, combined with the production and sampling, are among the reasons I hold ii in such high regard.

Seven perfect track ratings out of nine is quite an achievement in my personal rating system.

Even the two songs that received 90s are songs I rated highly.

Why Did I Give It 100 Instead of 97.78?

Here's where my review gets interesting.

Normally, I calculate an album's overall rating by averaging the individual tracks I've rated. I also tend to reserve a perfect overall score for albums where every rated song receives a 100.

But with ii, I've decided to make an exception.

The mathematical average is approximately 97.78, and I want to be transparent about that.

However, the overall listening experience impressed me so much that I decided to be generous and award the album a perfect 100.

This is a discretionary rating, not a claim that 97.78 mathematically rounds to 100.

Sometimes, the way I personally experience an album influences the final verdict beyond the numerical average.

And for this project, I believe that exception is worth making.

My Final Verdict

Denzel Curry and Kenneth Blume have created an album that I absolutely love.

Between the incredible production, epic sampling, strong guest appearances and my enjoyment of the songs themselves, ii has become one of the most memorable albums in my music-listening experience.

It isn't just my favorite album released in 2026.

It's my favorite album of the entire decade so far, and one of my all-time favorites.

My final rating?

100/100 — PERFECT!

That's my opinion, of course, and music is subjective. You might agree, disagree or fall somewhere in between.
