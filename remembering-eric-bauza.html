Remembering Eric Bauza: The Voice Behind Modern Looney Tunes Dies at 46

By Trace Thompkins | Trace The Rater
October 26, 2026

HEADS UP! Today's article is different from my usual entertainment reviews. We're taking a moment to remember an incredibly talented voice actor whose work brought laughter to cartoon fans around the world.

Sad News for the Animation Community 💔

On Sunday, October 25, 2026, Emmy Award-winning voice actor Eric Bauza passed away at the age of 46.

Bauza was widely known for voicing modern versions of legendary Looney Tunes characters, including Bugs Bunny, Daffy Duck, Porky Pig, Tweety Bird, and Marvin the Martian.

His family announced his sudden passing through his official Instagram account. According to the Los Angeles County Medical Examiner, his cause of death was hypertensive cardiovascular disease.

It's heartbreaking news for fans of animation, especially those who grew up enjoying the hilarious adventures of Bugs Bunny and his friends.

A Man of Many Cartoon Voices 🎙️

One of the most impressive things about Eric Bauza was his versatility.

Think about it! Voicing Bugs Bunny is already an enormous responsibility. But imagine also bringing Daffy Duck, Porky Pig, and several other recognizable cartoon personalities to life!

Bauza helped introduce these classic characters to modern audiences while respecting the comedic traditions established by earlier performers, particularly the legendary Mel Blanc.

His Looney Tunes work included:

  • Looney Tunes Cartoons
  • Space Jam: A New Legacy
  • Bugs Bunny Builders
  • The Day the Earth Blew Up: A Looney Tunes Movie
  • Coyote vs. Acme

But his talent extended beyond Looney Tunes!

Throughout his career, he contributed to productions associated with The Fairly OddParents, Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles, The Adventures of Puss in Boots, and many other animated franchises.

With more than 400 acting credits and three Children's & Family Emmy Awards, Bauza built an extraordinary career in voice acting.

Why His Work Matters to Animation 📺

Cartoons aren't just drawings moving across a screen.

The voices behind the characters are an essential part of what makes them memorable.

You can have fantastic animation, hilarious writing, and creative storytelling, but the voice performances help bring everything together!

And that's exactly what Eric Bauza accomplished.

Characters such as Bugs Bunny and Daffy Duck have entertained audiences for generations, and Bauza played an important role in continuing that tradition.

Whether you're a longtime Looney Tunes enthusiast or somebody discovering these characters for the first time, his performances are part of modern animation history.

My Thoughts as an Animation Fan ❤️

As somebody who absolutely loves cartoons, particularly classic animation, I find this news incredibly sad.

Looney Tunes has always represented some of the funniest and most creative entertainment around.

The comedic timing, expressive characters, ridiculous situations, and unforgettable voices are what make these cartoons legendary!

I strongly appreciate the performers who help preserve that entertainment for future generations.

Eric Bauza was one of those performers.

Although I never knew him personally, I appreciate the joy that his work brought to animation fans.

And while no performer can be replaced as a person, the characters and performances he contributed to will remain part of his legacy.

Rest in Peace, Eric Bauza 🕊️

My condolences go out to Eric Bauza's family, friends, colleagues, and everyone affected by his passing.

Thank you for the laughter, creativity, and unforgettable performances.

Your contributions to animation will continue to be appreciated by fans around the world.

Eric Bauza (1979–2026). Rest in peace. ❤️

This has been Trace The Rater, remembering a remarkable talent behind some of animation's most beloved characters.
