Our direction

One publishing home. Three editorial divisions.

Trace The Rater is an emerging, founder-led independent media venture. The website anchors a portfolio of written articles, long-form video, shorts, interviews and community features.

Entertainment & Pop Culture

Film, television, animation, music, streaming, reviews and creator interviews.

News & Community Journalism

Wichita-area culture, student life, local events and human-interest reporting.

Personal Commentary & Creator Stories

Media literacy, creative development and thoughtful personal essays.

The roadmap

Build carefully. Learn as we grow.

  1. Late 2026 · Foundation

    Improve the article archive, editorial workflow, portfolio, outreach and bookkeeping.

  2. 2027 · Expansion

    Develop repeatable formats, partnerships, freelance work and measured monetization tests.

  3. 2028 · Sustainability

    Strengthen recurring client relationships, financial resilience and a manageable workload.

Editorial independence comes first.

Verify claims, attribute sources, correct material errors, distinguish news from opinion, and disclose sponsorships and conflicts. Sponsors cannot purchase favorable reviews or dictate independent reporting conclusions.

The low-cost startup approach favors existing equipment and free tools. Potential revenue includes freelance services, eligible advertising, disclosed sponsorships and optional audience support.

A plan, not a performance report.

Targets, publishing ranges and budget allowances are proposed scenarios, not verified results or guaranteed earnings. Baselines will be established from actual data and reviewed quarterly. This plan does not claim the venture is incorporated, staffed, profitable or already monetized.
