MatPat and Stephanie Return to YouTube With PatChat: A New Chapter Worth Celebrating

By Trace Thompkins | Trace The Rater
November 17, 2026

Watch the video · Read the article

Watch: My thoughts on PatChat

After stepping away from his regular hosting duties on the Theorist channels in 2024, Matthew Patrick, better known as MatPat, is making YouTube videos together again with his wife and longtime creative partner, Stephanie Patrick. Their new channel is called PatChat.

The couple introduced PatChat on November 12, 2026, as a place to reconnect with viewers and share discoveries, stories, and topics that capture their curiosity. They plan to post videos when they have something they want to share, on a schedule that gives them time to work together and pursue other projects.

A Legacy of Entertainment and Education

MatPat became one of YouTube’s most recognizable creators through Game Theory, which began in 2011.

He and Stephanie founded Theorist Media, which grew to include Film Theory, Food Theory, and Style Theory. Together, these productions helped popularize an entertaining approach to examining popular culture through research, analysis, and storytelling.

His contributions demonstrate how online creators can combine educational subjects with engaging entertainment.

Stephanie also played an important role in developing the Theorist brand, contributing behind the scenes and appearing in content such as GTLive.

What Makes PatChat Different?

One of the most appealing aspects of PatChat is its flexibility. MatPat and Stephanie plan to publish videos when they have subjects they genuinely want to discuss.

In their announcement, the couple said they had spent time making movies, writing a musical, working with YouTube on AI policy, and getting involved in Washington, D.C.

The channel offers an opportunity for audiences to get to know the interests they’ve been pursuing since leaving Theorist.

My Perspective on Their Return

Personally, I am genuinely impressed by MatPat and Stephanie’s decision to create PatChat.

I’ve always respected MatPat’s contributions to the media and entertainment industry, especially his ability to make complicated subjects entertaining and accessible.

I also appreciate Stephanie’s contributions and am particularly enthusiastic about seeing her receive greater visibility alongside MatPat. I hope she continues to be a positive role model for viewers and aspiring creators.

As I continue developing Trace The Rater as an independent media and lifestyle journalism project, their decision also reinforces something I consider important. Creators should have the freedom to pursue meaningful ideas without allowing productivity demands to overshadow their creativity.

PatChat represents an encouraging example of established creators adapting their work to reflect changing personal and professional priorities.

Looking Ahead

The original Theorist channels continue under their respective creative teams. PatChat gives MatPat and Stephanie space to explore what interests them now.

Whether they’re discussing online trends, interesting experiences, or developments in entertainment, I look forward to seeing what they have planned.

Congratulations to MatPat and Stephanie on their new chapter!

What do you think about PatChat? Are you excited about their return to making videos together?
