Ranking the Five Types of Television: Which One Is the Best?

By Trace Thompkins
October 25, 2026

DING DING DING! It's Trace The Rater time! 📺😂

Television has entertained audiences for generations. Whether we're talking about cartoons, live-action sitcoms, action-adventure shows, or educational programming, there's something out there for almost everyone!

But today, I'm ranking five types of television based on my personal enjoyment!

The categories are Preschool Television, Youth & General Animation, Teen Live-Action, Adult Animation, and Adult Live-Action.

I'll be considering humor, storytelling, creativity, visuals, nostalgia, memorable characters, and overall entertainment!

HEADS UP! This is based on my personal preferences, not an objective judgment of every show within each category. Just because something ranks low doesn't necessarily mean it's bad!

Let's get started!

5. Preschool Television 🧸

Coming in last place is Preschool Television!

Now, obviously, I'm too old for most preschool programming. These shows are primarily designed to entertain and educate younger children, so they generally don't appeal to me as much anymore.

However, that doesn't mean everything in this category is bad!

For example, Bluey is a great show that demonstrates how preschool television can still entertain older audiences through its humor, characters, and storytelling.

So while this category ranks last for me, I can still appreciate what it has to offer!

4. Adult Live-Action 🎥

Coming in fourth place is Adult Live-Action!

This category is so much better for my personal interests than Preschool Television!

Although Adult Live-Action isn't my favorite type of television, there are still plenty of great shows to enjoy.

And my favorite adult live-action show? Seinfeld!

I especially appreciate the humor and memorable character interactions that make sitcoms like Seinfeld entertaining.

Adult Live-Action has a lot of variety, including sitcoms, dramas, and other types of programming.

Not my absolute favorite category, but still great!

Tied 2. Teen Live-Action 🎭

Now we're getting into some of my favorite types of television!

Teen Live-Action is fantastic because you really can't go wrong with many of these shows!

I'm especially interested in the special visuals, imaginative concepts, humor, and entertaining situations.

Some examples include My Two Dads, Wizards of Waverly Place, Best Friends Whenever, and Crash & Bernstein.

Whether we're talking about magical adventures, time-traveling teenagers, or hilarious interactions between human characters and puppets, there's a lot to enjoy!

I especially appreciate how shows like Wizards of Waverly Place and Best Friends Whenever incorporate fantasy and science-fiction concepts into live-action storytelling.

But there's another television category I consider equally entertaining!

Tied 2. Adult Animation 🌙

That's right! Adult Animation and Teen Live-Action are interchangeable in my ranking!

You really can't go wrong with either!

While Teen Live-Action can deliver impressive visuals and imaginative concepts, Adult Animation is incredibly fun to watch, especially when it comes to comedy.

Some of my favorites include Aqua Teen Hunger Force, The Simpsons, and Futurama!

These shows offer hilarious characters, ridiculous situations, and imaginative storytelling.

And just like youth and teen animation, Adult Animation can deliver entertaining adventures that keep me watching!

That's why I'm not placing Adult Animation definitively above or below Teen Live-Action.

They're equally enjoyable to me!

1. Youth & General Animation 🏆

DING DING DING! WE HAVE OUR WINNER!

YOUTH & GENERAL ANIMATION TAKES FIRST PLACE!

I mean, COME ON!

The Ren & Stimpy Show, Looney Tunes, Animaniacs, Gravity Falls, Phineas and Ferb, Avatar: The Last Airbender, SpongeBob SquarePants, and so many others?

LEGENDARY SHOWS!

This category offers just about everything I love about television!

You've got hilarious comedy, creative visuals, memorable characters, imaginative stories, action-packed adventures, and plenty of nostalgia!

For example, Ren & Stimpy offers outrageous cartoon humor, while Looney Tunes and Animaniacs deliver classic slapstick comedy.

Gravity Falls brings mystery and supernatural adventures, while Phineas and Ferb provides creative inventions and entertaining comedy.

And Avatar: The Last Airbender demonstrates how animation can tell compelling, emotional, action-packed stories.

Then there's SpongeBob SquarePants, which has delivered countless memorable characters and hilarious moments!

With so much variety, Youth & General Animation is easily my favorite category!

Final Thoughts

And there you have it! My ranking of the five types of television!

My final ranking:

  1. Youth & General Animation
  2. Teen Live-Action (tied)
  3. Adult Animation (tied)
  4. Adult Live-Action
  5. Preschool Television

For me, the best television experiences combine humor, entertaining stories, creativity, memorable visuals, nostalgia, and unforgettable characters.

Of course, different audiences have different tastes, and that's perfectly fine!

A category ranking lower doesn't mean it can't contain an amazing show. It simply means I enjoy other categories more.

And while Youth & General Animation takes first place, Teen Live-Action and Adult Animation are still incredible entertainment options!

What about you? Which type of television is your favorite?

This is Trace The Rater, and thanks for reading!
