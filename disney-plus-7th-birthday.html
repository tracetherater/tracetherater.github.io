Happy birthday, Disney+!

Today, November 12, 2026, marks the seventh anniversary of Disney+, one of the most recognizable streaming services in the entertainment industry.

Since its debut in 2019, Disney+ has evolved from a streaming destination centered on Disney's major entertainment brands into a broader platform featuring movies, television series, documentaries, original productions, and additional programming from Hulu and ESPN. Access varies by subscription and region.

Let's celebrate its history and examine how the streaming service has changed!

The Beginning: November 12, 2019

Disney+ officially launched on November 12, 2019, in the United States, Canada, and the Netherlands. Other markets followed on different dates.

At launch, Disney reported nearly 500 movies and 7,500 episodes of television programming. These are historical launch figures, not a count of today's library.

Its library featured entertainment from Disney, Pixar, Marvel, Star Wars, National Geographic, and other properties.

The launch also introduced original productions such as The Mandalorian, High School Musical: The Musical: The Series, Lady and the Tramp, and The Imagineering Story. Disney's launch announcement

The platform provided a new way for viewers to revisit familiar entertainment while discovering exclusive productions.

Seven Years of Evolution

Disney+ has changed substantially since 2019. The platform has expanded its original programming, introduced advertising-supported subscriptions, and developed closer connections with Hulu and ESPN.

Hulu on Disney+ officially launched for eligible U.S. subscribers on March 27, 2024. The ESPN hub followed on December 4, 2024. Hulu announcement · ESPN announcement

Disney currently describes limited Hulu and ESPN selections for standalone U.S. subscribers, with broader access through eligible subscriptions. Some Hulu content requires the Hulu app. Availability varies by region and plan. Current U.S. service details

These changes represent Disney's efforts to build a broader streaming experience. For viewers interested in multiple genres, the additional programming can offer more choices beyond Disney's traditional family entertainment.

What About the Subscription Prices?

One of the most significant changes involves pricing. When Disney+ originally launched, its monthly subscription cost $6.99 in the United States without advertisements. Original U.S. pricing

As checked for November 12, 2026, Disney lists standalone U.S. Disney+ with advertisements at $12.49 per month and Disney+ Premium at $21.49 per month. Select live and linear content may contain advertisements even on Premium. Bundles and promotional offers have separate prices and terms. Disney's current U.S. plans

These increases raise an important question: Is the growing entertainment library worth the higher subscription costs?

Different viewers will naturally have different answers depending on how frequently they use the service and what fits their budget.

What Makes Disney+ Special? My Take

One of Disney+'s defining characteristics is its combination of established franchises, classic entertainment, and newer original productions.

From animated classics to live-action adventures, viewers can explore multiple generations of storytelling through one service.

For Trace The Rater, I believe both animation and live-action programming deserve appreciation.

I believe a streaming platform should make it convenient to discover entertaining programs, revisit older favorites, and experience something new. Preserving classic programming is especially important to me as the entertainment industry continues changing.

Looking Ahead

As Disney+ begins its eighth year, it will be interesting to see how the platform continues evolving.

Will it introduce more original productions? Improve content discovery? Expand its library? Make subscriptions more affordable?

Only time will tell.

Regardless of what the future holds, November 12, 2026, represents a meaningful milestone in the history of modern streaming entertainment.

Happy seventh birthday, Disney+!
