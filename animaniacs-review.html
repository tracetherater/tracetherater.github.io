Animaniacs Review: My Favorite Show of All Time

By Trace Thompkins | Trace The Rater
November 21, 2026

Watch the review · Read the review

Watch: My Animaniacs Review

DING DING DING! It’s Trace The Rater time! Today I’m reviewing Animaniacs, a cartoon packed with wild characters, memorable songs, and all kinds of adventures.

The Warner siblings—Yakko, Wakko, and Dot—escape from the Warner Bros. water tower and bring their chaos to the studio and everyone around them. The show also jumps between a whole cast of characters and different kinds of stories. Pinky and the Brain are always cooking up schemes, Slappy Squirrel and her nephew Skippy get into trouble, and the Goodfeathers, Buttons and Mindy, Rita and Runt, Katie Ka-Boom, Chicken Boo, and the Hip Hippos each bring their own flavor to the show.

I decided to revisit Animaniacs after it started streaming on Tubi in March. That gave me a chance to pick up where I had left off on other platforms and keep watching more episodes. And I’m glad I did!

The Animation Is Incredible

The animation is excellent. The show is vibrant, expressive, and full of energy. Every scene feels alive, whether the characters are bouncing around the studio, getting tangled up in a scheme, or launching into a musical number. Honestly, this is probably the best animation I’ve ever seen in my life.

A Cast With Its Own Kind of Chaos

Every character is good in their own way, and each one has a distinct personality and a different kind of adventure. The Warner siblings can turn almost anything into a hilarious mess. Pinky and the Brain bring their big plans and even bigger mishaps. Slappy and Skippy, the Goodfeathers, Buttons and Mindy, and the rest of the crew all have their own style of comedy.

Even when the characters get into trouble, you can see what makes each of them special. That variety keeps the show feeling fresh from one segment to the next.

Stories That Make Me Laugh—and Feel Something

The stories change with each segment, which is a big part of the fun. One moment the show is going completely wild; the next, it finds a surprisingly warm or emotional note.

Animaniacs is the funniest show I have ever watched. The Warner siblings’ antics make me laugh, while stories like Rita and Runt’s can move into darker territory. Their care for each other—even when mistakes get made—gives those segments a heartfelt quality that stays with me.

Music That Sticks With You

The music is classic. I especially love the geography songs, the theme songs, and the Warner siblings’ recitals about the things they encounter. The songs are catchy, clever, and a perfect fit for the show’s lively personality. They’re the kind of tunes that keep playing in your head long after the episode ends.

My Favorite Show of All Time

So, after looking at all of this, what do I actually think?

Animaniacs is my favorite show of all time, and Wakko is my personal idol! The show brings together a wide variety of entertaining segments, lovable and hilarious characters, excellent animation, memorable stories, and music I never get tired of hearing. It can make me laugh out loud and then surprise me with a genuinely heartwarming moment.

Trace The Rater Score: 10/10

My score for Animaniacs is 10/10.

There is so much to enjoy: the variety of content, the memorable characters, the beautiful animation, the funny and touching storylines, and the iconic music. Every part of the show contributes to why it means so much to me. It earns a perfect score and a place as my favorite show of all time.
